# Meeting QA

## Use

Keep the room's outputs useful, inspectable, and not just conversational.

## Inputs

- Current brief
- Task Center items
- Team Inbox discoveries
- Stage objective

## Method

1. Check whether the team has a decision, an owner, and a next artifact.
2. Convert vague agreement into a concrete decision or open question.
3. Ask for a structured tool artifact when it would reduce ambiguity.
4. Close the stage only when the next action is clear.

## Output

- One decision
- One next action
- One unresolved risk or question

## QA Gate

The meeting should leave behind something a user can inspect after the chat scrolls away.
